Julius Gassert

PhD Student in Astrophysics · University Observatory Munich (USM / LMU)

I am a PhD student in Daniel Gruen's group at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München. My work focuses on optical follow-up of gravitational-wave events with the 2m Fraunhofer Telescope at Wendelstein Observatory, as well as machine learning methods for gravitational-wave population inference.
